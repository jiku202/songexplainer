"Anak" is a Filipino song by Freddie Aguilar from 1978, sung in Tagalog. It became one of the most widely recognized songs in Philippine music and was later covered and translated in many languages and countries.

Themes: the bond between parent and child, a child's choices and their consequences, and parental love that continues through disappointment.

Readings listeners offer: a parent's lament for a child who has drifted away; a child's later realization of what their parents gave; a universal story about family tension and forgiveness.
