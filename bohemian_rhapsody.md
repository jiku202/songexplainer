---
title: Bohemian Rhapsody
artist: Queen
year: 1975
tags: rock opera, narrative, confession, progressive, theatrical
---
"Bohemian Rhapsody" was written by Freddie Mercury and released in 1975 on A Night at the Opera. It has no repeating chorus. Instead it moves through distinct sections: a ballad, an operatic middle, a hard-rock passage, and a quiet ending.

Mercury was famously reluctant to give a detailed explanation of the lyrics, so most readings are listener interpretation rather than documented intent.

Readings listeners offer: a confession by someone who has done something irreversible and faces the consequences; a coded expression of personal turmoil and identity; a deliberately theatrical collage of characters and styles that is meant to be enjoyed as drama.

Its sectional structure is often compared to opera, which makes it a good example of form shaping meaning.
