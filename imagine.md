"Imagine" is the title track of John Lennon's 1971 solo album. It is built on a gentle piano pattern and a calm vocal, which contrasts with the bold ideas in the words.

Lennon said the song was partly inspired by conceptual instruction poems in Yoko Ono's book Grapefruit. In 2017 Yoko Ono was officially added as a co-writer.

Themes: world peace, shared humanity, and picturing a world without borders, rigid possessions, or divisions drawn by religion and nationalism.

Readings listeners offer: a hopeful peace anthem; a challenge to institutions that divide people; a thought experiment that invites the listener to imagine rather than a set of demands. Critics have also pointed out the tension between its message and the wealth of its famous author.
