"Ang Huling El Bimbo" is a song by the Filipino band Eraserheads, released in 1995 on the album Cutterpillow and written by vocalist Ely Buendia. It is widely regarded as an OPM classic.

The song looks back on youth and a school-days dance, mixing playful memories with a sense of loss as time moves on.

Readings listeners offer: nostalgia for school days and a first love; a story about something being lost or changed for good as people grow up; a song that sounds light and catchy while carrying a sadder undertone.
