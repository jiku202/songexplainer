---
title: Born to Run
artist: Bruce Springsteen
year: 1975
tags: escape, youth, working class, rock, romance
---
"Born to Run" is the title track of Bruce Springsteen's 1975 album and his commercial breakthrough. Its dense, big-sounding production was reportedly crafted over a long studio process.

Themes: escaping a small town, youthful restlessness, and the mix of romance and desperation in chasing a better life.

Readings listeners offer: a celebration of freedom and youth; a love song in which two people try to escape together; a more bittersweet reading that senses the cost or the uncertainty of the escape.

Springsteen often writes about working-class characters caught between hope and limited options, a thread that connects this song to much of his catalog.
