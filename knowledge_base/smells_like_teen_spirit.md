"Smells Like Teen Spirit" was released in 1991 on Nirvana's album Nevermind and was written mainly by Kurt Cobain. The title is widely reported to come from a deodorant brand that a friend referenced in graffiti.

Themes: apathy, alienation, and frustration with mass culture, often linked to Generation X.

Readings listeners offer: an anthem of youthful disillusionment; a satirical take on being turned into a rallying cry for a generation; a song whose loud-quiet dynamic expresses the tension between numbness and anger.

The lyrics are deliberately fragmentary, so many listeners respond more to the sound and mood than to a single story.
