title: Bayan Ko

"Bayan Ko" is a Filipino patriotic song whose words were written by Jose Corazon de Jesus, with music by Constancio de Guzman, in 1928. It was composed as a kundiman, a traditional Filipino love song form, but its subject is love of country and longing for freedom during American colonial rule.

In later decades it became strongly associated with opposition to the Marcos dictatorship and with the 1986 EDSA People Power Revolution, when it was widely sung as an anthem of resistance.

Readings listeners offer: a lament for a homeland held captive; a love song in which the beloved is the nation; a reminder of how an old song can take on new political meaning in a new era.
