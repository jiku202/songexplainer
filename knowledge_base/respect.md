artist: Aretha Franklin

tags: empowerment, soul, civil rights, feminism, cover version

"Respect" was written and first recorded by Otis Redding in 1965. Aretha Franklin's 1967 recording reshaped it with new musical elements and a spelled-out hook, along with backing vocals, and it became her signature song.

Themes: demanding dignity and fair treatment in a relationship.

Franklin's version was adopted as an anthem by the civil rights and women's movements of the era, although it was not written as a political statement.

Readings listeners offer: a personal relationship song; a broader declaration of self-worth; a good example of how a cover can change a song's meaning by changing the singer's perspective.
