"Fix You" appears on Coldplay's 2005 album X&Y. It is a slow-building ballad that starts with a church-organ sound and quiet vocals, then grows into a fuller, guitar-driven finish.

Frontman Chris Martin has been widely reported to have written it to comfort his then-partner Gwyneth Paltrow after the death of her father. Treat this as commonly reported background rather than a full account of the song's meaning.

Themes: offering comfort to someone who is hurting, the limits of being able to "fix" another person's pain, and staying present during grief.

Readings listeners offer: a direct song of consolation to one person; a wider message to anyone feeling lost or worn out; a song whose swelling arrangement mirrors the move from despair toward hope.
