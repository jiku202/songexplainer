"Heal the World" is from Michael Jackson's 1991 album Dangerous. It opens with a children's voice and moves into a soft, uplifting pop ballad. In 1992 Jackson founded the Heal the World Foundation, which focused on helping children.

Themes: compassion, caring for children, and the idea that individual kindness can add up to a better world.

Readings listeners offer: a sincere humanitarian appeal; a sentimental, idealistic vision some find naive; a song about responsibility toward the next generation.

Compared with "Imagine," it is more about action and care for others than about abolishing the structures that divide people.
